## Comparison notes

- **Overlaps** (brief ∩ risk map): priority leads — both parties worried
  about them.
- **Brief-only items**: verify, but ask why they were not on your map.
- **Map-only items** (things the brief never mentions): not automatically
  defects, but candidate blind spots — gaps in the implementer's "audit
  closely" list are themselves signal.
